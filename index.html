Frequently asked questions

How do I start learning?

Open the app, choose a learning category, and select a card or available lesson. Follow the on-screen steps to study and practice.

Where can I review completed lessons?

Open the Review list from the main screen to revisit completed lessons.

Can I turn off sound or spoken cards?

Yes. Open Settings to adjust sound and automatic card reading.

How do I report a bug?

Use the support link above to create a GitHub issue. Include the app version, iOS version, and steps to reproduce the problem. Do not publish passwords, private account details, or other sensitive information.

Need more help?

For app issues and feedback, visit the KivyBee support repository. GitHub may require an account to create an issue. Do not post sensitive personal information publicly.
